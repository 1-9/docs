title: "Messenger Settings"

---

**Settings → Messengers** controls the **Contact Messengers** widget — direct WhatsApp / Telegram buttons available on the Default template and any [Landing Page](/domains/landing-pages).

## WhatsApp

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Enabled** | Turns the WhatsApp button on site-wide. |
| **Number** | Digits only, with country code, no `+` or spaces — e.g. `15551234567`. |
| **Pre-filled Message** | Shown in the visitor's WhatsApp compose box before they send it. Use `{{domainName}}` as a placeholder — substituted per domain. |

## Telegram

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Enabled** | Turns the Telegram button on site-wide. |
| **Username** | Without the `@` symbol. |
| **Pre-filled Message** | Same as WhatsApp — supports `{{domainName}}`. |

## How it protects your number from scraping

Your WhatsApp number and Telegram username are never written into the page's HTML or any script the browser downloads. The button links to the platform's own domain, not directly to `wa.me`/`t.me` — clicking it hits a same-origin redirect that looks up your real contact info on the server and forwards the visitor on. A bot scanning your page source for phone numbers or usernames finds nothing to harvest.

<Info>
  There's no CAPTCHA, no rate limiter, and no contact form to protect here — the button is a plain outbound link, not a form submission, so there's nothing for a spam bot to submit in the first place. The only thing worth protecting is the contact value itself, and that's handled by never exposing it.
</Info>

## Hiding it on specific domains

Enabling a channel here turns it on site-wide. To hide the whole widget on one domain — a premium listing you're handling personally through a different channel, for example — check **Hide Contact Messengers** in that domain's sidebar. There's no per-channel override; it's all or nothing per domain, matching how the widget is actually used.

## Where it appears

**Contact Messengers** is a sidebar widget block, like Inquiry Form or Marketplace Buttons — add, remove, or reorder it from **Templates → Domain** (or a specific [Landing Page](/domains/landing-pages)'s Right Column). See [Templates](/content/templates#sidebar-widgets).
