title: "General Settings"

---

**Settings → General** contains the foundational identity of your marketplace — the fields that appear in the browser tab, the site header, footer, and search results.

## Identity

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Site Name** | Your marketplace name. Used in the browser tab title and SEO metadata. |
| **Tagline** | Short one-liner shown in the site header or hero section depending on your template. |
| **Logo** | Uploaded image used in the site header. SVG or PNG with transparent background recommended. |
| **Favicon** | Small icon shown in the browser tab and bookmarks. PNG or ICO, ideally 32×32 or 64×64 px. |

## Contact

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Contact Email** | Shown on the site for general inquiries. Not the same as the notification email in [Notification Settings](/configuration/notification-settings). |
| **Phone** | Optional. Shown if your template includes a contact section. |
| **Address** | Optional. Shown in the footer if set. |

## Footer

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Footer Text** | Rich text block shown in the upper footer area. Good for a brief about statement or newsletter description. |
| **Copyright Text** | Shown in the bottom bar of the footer. The current year and your site name are prepended automatically — just add the suffix, e.g. `All rights reserved.` |

## Social Links

Add links to your social profiles — Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Telegram, and others. Each entry is a platform + URL pair. Enabled platforms are displayed as icon links in the site footer.

Supported platforms: Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Telegram, GitHub, Other.
